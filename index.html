Paper Cuts.

Take turns slicing the sheet. The player who makes the last cut wins.

How to play

The sheet is a grid of unit squares. On your turn, hover near any internal grid line inside a piece — a red blade guide appears — and click to cut all the way across that piece, splitting it in two.

  • You can only cut along internal lines, never the outer border.
  • Every cut splits exactly one region into two, so every offered cut is valid.
  • The game ends when every piece is a 1×1 square. Whoever made the final cut wins.
  • "Pre-cuts" starts the sheet with some cuts already made, as the rules allow.
